## 6. ISBN & Legal Compliance  
- ISBN: Required before publication  
- Copyright: Register with relevant national authority  
- Child Safety: Compliance with child media privacy and safety laws  

---

## 7. Printing & Format Details  
- Trim size: 8 x 10 inches  
- Paper type: Matte finish, FSC certified  
- Binding: Perfect binding  
- Color: Full-color CMYK  
- Quantity: 1,000 copies

## 8. Marketing & Distribution Plan  
- Channels: Educational institutions, libraries, parenting groups, online bookstores, social media  
- Strategy: Target educators and parents via sample copies and promotional material; engage parenting blogs and social media  

---

## 9. Post-Launch Activities  
- Collect feedback from customers and educators  
- Plan for further print runs or digital editions  
- Consider translation or adaptation opportunities  

---

## 10. Risk Management  
- Risks: Illustration delays, budget overruns, print quality issues  
- Strategies: Set clear deadlines/check-ins, maintain contingency budgets, use reputable printers
