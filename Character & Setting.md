# Character & Setting Guide: The Umbrella Who Wanted Sunshine

## Characters

### Blue Umbrella
- **Role:** Protagonist
- **Description:**
A cheerful, curious blue umbrella who dreams of seeing the sun despite being designed for rainy days. It possesses a hopeful and reflective nature, eager to discover more about the world beyond the rain. Visually, it features a bright blue canopy with a rounded handle and sturdy ribs, symbolizing protection and resilience.

## Settings

### Rainy Town
- **Description:**
A whimsical, gentle town often experiencing rainy weather. Streets sparkle with raindrops, and puddles mirror a spectrum of colorful umbrellas. The atmosphere is cozy and soft, infused with the calming sounds of gentle rain and occasional peeks of sunlight that break through the cloud cover.

### Sunlit Clearing
- **Description:**
An open, inviting clearing that appears during sunny spells. Bathed in golden light, it bursts with vivid colors brought to life by blooming flowers and active small creatures. This bright environment contrasts with the rainy town and symbolizes warmth, hope, and discovery.

## Environment & Atmosphere
The story’s settings shift between the soothing, textured rainy scenes filled with gentle sounds and wet earth scents, and the vibrant, warm sunny moments glowing with golden sunlight and lively wildlife. These alternating environments highlight the narrative’s themes of appreciating diverse situations and recognizing beauty everywhere.
